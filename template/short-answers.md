Keep answers short: the result first, then only what is needed. No status tables, no step-by-step story of what was tried. Questions go at the end, numbered, so they are easy to answer.

**Why:** long reports take time to read and hide what matters.

**How to apply:** every answer, in every conversation.

Example note of type `feedback`: a rule the user gave about how to work. Notes of this type are always followed.
