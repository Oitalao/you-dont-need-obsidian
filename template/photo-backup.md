Phone photos sync with the `~/Pictures/Phone` folder on the computer, which is copied every night to an external drive. The drive has 2 TB; on 2026-10-28 it had 1.4 TB in use.

Check the free space once a month. Above 90%, tell the user before the copy stops working.
